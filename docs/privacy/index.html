Overworld

Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Overworld has no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your saved places, search history and settings stay on your device. "Clean my data" in Settings deletes them.

Location

Your location is used on your device to show where you are and to guide you. It is only sent to a service when it's needed to answer a request, as described below. Overworld never stores or sells it.

Services the app talks to

  • Map tiles from OpenFreeMap (tiles.openfreemap.org): the map area you are viewing.
  • Search with Photon (photon.komoot.io) and Nominatim (nominatim.openstreetmap.org): what you type, and an approximate position to rank nearby results.
  • Routing with OSRM (router.project-osrm.org): the start and end points of a route.

These services receive your device's IP address like any website does, and each has its own privacy policy. Overworld doesn't send them any identifier.

Sharing

When you share a place, the link contains its coordinates and name. Opening the link loads a page from this site, which shows an OpenStreetMap map of that place.

Car displays

With CarPlay or Android Auto, the app passes the same information to your car's display. It stays on your phone and in the car.
